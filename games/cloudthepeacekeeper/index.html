Cloud the Peacekeeper

You are a cloud drifting over a one-street town. People wander in from both sides. Some carry trouble. Your only power is lightning — but a strike kills. A near miss only scares folk away. Keep people alive long enough and grateful builders will start raising a monument. Finish it to win.

How to play
  • Move the mouse to drift the cloud.
  • Click (or press Space) to strike lightning where you point.
  • Direct hit → that citizen falls (a death you should avoid).
  • Near miss → citizens flee away from the bolt, faster.
  • Medics revive the fallen if they reach them in time.
  • Assassins & cowboys harm others; knights are protected.
  • Avoid permanent deaths to grow your combo and summon builders.
  • Press M to mute, P to pause.
