🚇 Metrordle

A daily-style deduction puzzle: guess the secret transit station. Each guess tells you how many lines it shares with the target and which direction to head along the map — narrow it down from the whole system. Play the Washington Metro or Philadelphia, and toggle which lines are in play.

⛈️ Cloud the Peacekeeper

You're a cloud drifting over a one-street town. People wander in from both sides and some carry trouble. Your only power is lightning — but a direct strike kills, while a near miss merely scares troublemakers off. Keep people alive long enough and grateful builders raise a monument; finish it to win. Pick your cloud and the town's mood, then drift in.
