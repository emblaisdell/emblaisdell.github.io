Hello, I'm Eben! I'm a mathematical logician who loves bringing ideas into the real world.

Professionally, I work for MITRE as a senior cyber security researcher, helping defenders bring new tools to bear against real threats. More broadly, I work on applying AI/ML to NP-complete problems, domains where solutions are hard to come by but failure and validation are both cheap.

Personally, I have lots of fun small ideas and love getting to make them. Here are some!
